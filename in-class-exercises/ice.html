Favorite Devotional

This ICE challenge presents a table of your favorite devotionals with a background image of the i-Center from the BYU-Idaho campus. It also provides a form where you can enter your own favorite devotionals.

To me, the challenge served as an introduction to tables and how to set them up and organize them. It also served as an introduction to forms and how they can interactively add data to a table.

Signup

In this project, you will find a (mockup) sign-up sheet for a Daily Scripture Feed newsletter, where you would hypothetically recieve an email or text every day with the daily verse from a catalogue of all the Standard Works of the Church.
